Getting Here

Airport Information

For those traveling from further away, we recommend the following airports:

PHL — Philadelphia International
Approximately 60 minutes driving to the venue

EWR — Newark Liberty International
Approximately 70 minutes driving to the venue

LGA — LaGuardia International
Approximately 100 minutes driving to the venue

JFK — John F. Kennedy International
Approximately 120 minutes driving to the venue

Regional Transportation

From New York City / Newark
NJ Transit is available from New York Penn Station and Newark Penn Station. If you are taking NJ Transit, you can disembark at Hamilton Train Station.

From Philadelphia
SEPTA is available from PHL. If you are taking SEPTA, you can disembark at Doylestown Train Station.

Rideshare / Car Rentals
Rideshare apps are widely available in the area. Car rentals are also a great option for guests interested in exploring Bucks County.
