Attire

We are so excited to celebrate with you throughout the wedding weekend. For those wondering what to wear, please see below for a little guidance. Overall, we want everyone to feel beautiful, comfortable, and festive — there is no strict color palette for guests.

Welcome Dinner

Cocktail attire.
Think bright summer colors, fun patterns, dresses or skirts for women, and slacks with a dress shirt for men. Jackets are welcome but completely optional.

Wedding Day

Black tie.
For women, we suggest long dresses or gowns. For men, tuxedos are encouraged, though a dark suit is also perfectly welcome.

For the wedding day, we would love for guests to embrace a range of colors. The bridal party will be wearing green, so if you are deciding between options, we encourage other summer shades. That said, if you have a green dress you love, please wear it — the most important thing is that you feel your best.

Farewell Brunch

Semi-formal summer attire.
Think sundresses, skirts, or summer dresses for women, and pants with a linen or dress shirt for men.

Please do not feel boxed in by this guidance. Wear what makes you feel confident and ready to celebrate — we cannot wait to see everyone.
