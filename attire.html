Attire

We are so excited to celebrate with you throughout the wedding weekend. For those wondering what to wear, please see below for some guidance.

Welcome Reception

Cocktail attire. Think long or short dresses for women. Think slacks with a dress shirt for men - jackets are welcome but completely optional.

Wedding Ceremony and Reception

Black tie attire. For the wedding day, we would love for guests to embrace a range of colors. For women, we suggest long dresses or gowns. For men, tuxedos are encouraged.
Note: Our wedding ceremony will take place outdoors (weather permitting), so we recommend wearing or bringing a change of footwear that is comfortable on grass.

Farewell Brunch

Garden party attire. Think summer dresses or skirts for women, and pants with a linen or dress shirt for men.
