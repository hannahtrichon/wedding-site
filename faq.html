Good to Know

Can I bring a guest or my children?

We absolutely adore all of your significant others and little ones! To help us accommodate everyone comfortably, we kindly ask that you please refer to your invitation or RSVP page for your invited guest list. If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out.

What should I wear?

Please visit the Attire page for dress code guidance for each event throughout the weekend.

Where can I find parking information?

Parking information for each event, including shuttle details for the Welcome Reception, can be found on the Events page.

What if I need transportation?

Transportation details, including shuttle service for Friday and Saturday, can be found on the Events page.

Where should I stay?

Hotel room blocks, Airbnb & VRBO recommendations, and additional nearby accommodations can all be found on the Travel page.

Which airport should I fly into?

Our recommended airports and transportation information can be found on the Travel page.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

Our ceremony is planned to take place outdoors (weather permitting), while the reception will be a combination of indoor and outdoor spaces.

Will the ceremony be unplugged?

We kindly ask that phones and cameras remain tucked away during the ceremony so our photographer and videographer can capture the moment without distraction. We promise to share all of the beautiful photos afterward! Please feel free to take photos throughout the rest of the weekend - we'd love to see them.

What time should I arrive?

We recommend arriving approximately 15 minutes before the start of each event to allow plenty of time to park, get settled, and find your seat.

How do I RSVP?

Please visit the RSVP page to submit your response. We can't wait to celebrate with you!

Still have a question?

Please don't hesitate to reach out. We're always happy to help and want everyone to have a wonderful weekend celebrating with us.
