Saturday, September 12, 2026

Wedding Ceremony & Reception

Hotel du Village
Guest Arrival 5:00 PM
Ceremony 5:30 PM
Reception to follow

Attire

Black tie attire. For the wedding day, we would love for guests to embrace a range of colors. For women, we suggest floor length dresses or gowns. For men, tuxedos are encouraged.
Our wedding ceremony will take place outdoors, weather permitting, so we recommend wearing or bringing a change of footwear that is comfortable on grass.

For guests in need of a tuxedo rental, our family can coordinate your tuxedo rental at a discounted rate of $249 including shoes. If you would like to be included, please send your measurements to Hannah as soon as possible. If you need your measurements taken, you can visit your local formal wear shop. Please reach out with any questions

For guests driving

Complimentary parking is available at Hotel du Village for wedding guests. Please plan to arrive approximately 15 minutes before the ceremony begins to allow time to park and find your seat.

For guests utilizing transportation

Transportation will be available from River House at Odette’s at 4:15 PM, Logan Inn at 4:30 PM and The Stockton Inn at 4:30 PM to Hotel du Village for the ceremony and reception.
